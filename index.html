About Madhi Studio

Your business has something worth sharing. I help you bring it into focus through thoughtful design, distinctive imagery, and clear words—so people can understand what you offer and know where to go next.

Madhi Studio is my independent creative practice, bringing together branding, interface design, motion graphics, websites, photography, and copywriting. I work with small businesses, independent professionals, and organizations that want to communicate with more clarity and character.

My goal is to understand your audience, identify what the project actually needs, and build it well. That might mean a logo with useful variations, a focused landing page, an interactive graphic, or a better combination of imagery and words. We shape the work around your purpose, scope, and budget.

The examples below show different ways I can help you present your business, explain an idea, and make your message easier to understand.

Interactive Location Map

Need to show where you operate or help people explore your locations? I can connect a map with labels and location details, so selecting one helps people find the other. This approach can support branch finders, service areas, venue guides, or location-based information.
In this featured work, WanderPorts Airport Network connects an interactive world map with airport-code labels so users can locate and explore major U.S. and international airports visually.

Guided Workflow Interface

Need to walk people through a process? I can design an interface with step-by-step progress, focused views, and useful controls such as start, pause, and reset. It can help explain inspections, demonstrations, training sequences, or other tasks where people need to understand both the current step and the whole process.
In this featured work, an interactive runway inspection prototype uses a moving detailed viewport, a synchronized full-runway overview, and simple controls to guide users through an airfield inspection process. Start, pause, or reset the inspection and follow progress across the runway.

Interactive Visual Diagram

Need people to explore an arrangement or understand a plan? I can create a responsive diagram with selectable elements, clear labels, and visual feedback. The same approach can support room layouts, team structures, equipment diagrams, or other information that is easier to understand visually.
In this featured work, Interactive Soccer Pitch turns a field layout into a responsive visual diagram that can support positions, formations, and selectable information.

How I Work.

Small projects work best when the decisions stay visible. We agree on the goal, build the strongest useful version, review what matters, and launch it.

Define.A Clear Scope.

Agree on the audience, purpose, pages, content, functions, and what the finished project needs to accomplish.

Design.A Working Site.

Create the layout, visual language, responsive behavior, and interactions as one coherent working experience.

Build.A Launch-Ready Version.

Test the important details, gather focused feedback, and resolve the changes that improve clarity or function.

Launch.A Powerful Communications Medium

Publish the site, verify it in the real environment, and leave the structure ready for future pages and projects.

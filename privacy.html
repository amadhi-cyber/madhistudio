Privacy Policy

Updated October 10, 2026

This policy describes how Madhi Studio handles the information you choose to send through this website, including project inquiries, attachments, and newsletter signup requests.

Who we are

Madhi Studio is an independent creative practice serving clients through branding, user interface design, motion graphics, website design, photography, and copywriting. For questions about privacy, contact info@madhistudio.com.

Information you provide

When you use the contact form, we may receive your name, email address, organization, website address, project type, timeline, message, and any files you upload. Newsletter signup requests provide an email address and your affirmative request to receive studio updates. Please avoid sending passwords, payment card numbers, government identifiers, or other sensitive information through these forms.

How we use information

We use inquiry information to evaluate your request, reply, prepare estimates, communicate about possible projects, and maintain relevant business records. Newsletter contact information is used only for studio updates you have requested. We do not sell submitted contact information or use inquiry submissions to automatically enroll you in marketing emails.

Form processing and external services

Website forms are processed through FormSubmit, which transmits submissions to our designated business email account. FormSubmit and the email provider may process submission content and technical information to deliver messages and prevent abuse. Files attached to a project inquiry are also transmitted through this service. Public website resources, such as remotely hosted fonts, images, and other third-party assets, may cause your browser to contact their providers directly.

Device storage, cookies, and analytics

The current site uses local browser storage to remember a motion/display preference. We have not intentionally installed Google Analytics, advertising pixels, behavioral advertising cookies, or a separate visitor-tracking service in the reviewed website files. Third-party services or browsers may handle technical data independently. See our Cookies page for details.

Retention and security

We keep communications and project material only for as long as reasonably needed to handle inquiries, deliver agreed work, maintain legitimate business records, or meet legal obligations. Reasonable safeguards are used, but no email or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. If you want to request access, correction, or deletion of information you submitted, email us; requests will be considered subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

Newsletter choices

Signing up is voluntary. You can stop receiving studio emails at any time by using the unsubscribe method provided in a message, or by emailing info@madhistudio.com with an unsubscribe request. We do not send a newsletter merely because you submitted a project inquiry.

Children, changes, and contact

This site is intended for a general business audience, not directed to children under 13. We may update this policy when our services, forms, analytics, checkout, or legal obligations change. The updated version and effective date will appear here. Privacy questions: info@madhistudio.com.

Interactive Airport Network

An interactive airport network visualization combining major U.S. and international airport locations, airport-code cross-highlighting, and visual airport browsing.

Interaction

Hover airport dots or airport codes to cross-highlight locations. The streamlined demo uses 10 U.S. and 10 international airports; hover the image reel to pause it.

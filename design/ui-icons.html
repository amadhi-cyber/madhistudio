An interactive airport network visualization combining major U.S. and international airport locations, airport-code cross-highlighting, and visual airport browsing. Hover airport dots or airport codes to cross-highlight locations. The streamlined demo uses 10 U.S. and 10 international airports; hover the image reel to pause it.

This game teaches kids the A to Z of airports while building their vocabulary, improving pattern recognition and spelling. Find all 26 hidden A to Z airport words in the grid. Drag across each word, or tap its first and last letters. Words may run forward, backward, vertical, horizontal or diagonal. Correct words highlight and cross off automatically. Good luck!

An interactive runway inspection prototype with a moving detailed viewport and synchronized full-runway overview. Start the runway check, pause or reset the inspection, follow progress in the runway overview, and use the built-in full-screen control for a larger inspection view.

A visual learning system that turns real airport ideas into age-friendly explainers, games, and activities, moving kids from curiosity to understanding through active exploration.

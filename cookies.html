What this website stores

The website currently uses browser localStorage to remember whether you prefer motion effects enabled or disabled. This is a device/browser preference, not an advertising identifier. It is stored locally so your setting can persist between visits.

Analytics and advertising cookies

The October 10, 2026 website build reviewed for this policy does not contain a deliberate installation of Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, advertising cookies, or a separate visitor analytics platform. If any optional tracking is added in future, this page and any necessary consent choices must be updated before activation.

Third-party services and technical information

Some fonts, images, and forms rely on outside providers. Your browser may make ordinary network requests to these providers, and they may separately process technical connection information. The contact and newsletter forms use FormSubmit. See the Privacy Policy for more information.

How to control storage

You may clear this site’s stored data in your browser settings, block cookies where supported, or use private browsing. Disabling storage may reset the motion preference when you return. These controls are managed in your browser.
